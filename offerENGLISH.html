WHAT I OFFER

Documentary Film

I have been working in documentary film since 2015. I focus on auteur documentary filmmaking, creating both television and independent films. I have also produced several short documentaries for the non-profit organization Inventura. Currently, I am working on my debut feature-length documentary film (produced by M3, s.r.o.) about the glass ceiling (the invisible barriers that prevent women from advancing professionally). The development of the project has been supported by the Czech Film Fund.

Audio Documentary

I have been creating audio documentaries since 2016. I have produced work for Radio Wave, Czech Radio Dvojka, Vltava, Czech Radio Plus, and the platform Hatefree Culture. Thanks to the anonymity that sound as a medium offers, my audio documentaries open up taboo and intimate topics.

Commercial Audiovisual Content

I welcome any professional commissions in the field of documentary production. My work always retains a documentary character, but I am flexible and able to adapt to different assignments. I strongly welcome new challenges, and empathy is one of my key strengths.

Education

I have teaching experience at FAMO in Písek and at the Department of Film and Theatre Studies at the Faculty of Arts, Palacký University in Olomouc. I can teach practice-oriented classes in audio and documentary production (I have professional experience from Czech Television, Czech Radio, the festival environment, independent filmmaking, and work for the non-profit sector).

Journalism

For five years I worked on the environmental current affairs programme Nedej se for ČT2 and for four years on the programme Průvan for ČT Art. I would like to continue working in journalistic production.

Streaming

I produced a live stream for the Documentary Film Centre (MFDF Jihlava) and I would like to continue developing my skills in this creative field.

Consulting in Documentary Production

I am happy to advise those working on an audio or film documentary who would like consultation at any stage of the creative process. I can share my know-how and support the creation of new projects.
