Czech Woman 2028



The upcoming film CZECH WOMAN is a film about the working lives of contemporary women in the Czech Republic. The film will consist of several chapters that chronologically follow a woman’s life from birth to old age.




Today’s motherhood is a product of modern times. For mothers, it is often considered a success if they have a grandmother nearby who is retired, the financial means to afford a babysitter, or a husband working from home. However, few people have this privilege, and families often have fewer children than they would like. WHY?


Among the most common problems faced by contemporary Czech mothers are:


  • Lack of places in childcare facilities
  • Financial pressure and insecurity
  • Exhaustion and lack of time for themselves
  • Social isolation and loneliness
  • Balancing work and family life (work flexibility)
  • Pressure to achieve “perfect motherhood” and feelings of failure
  • Limited access to healthcare, psychological, and institutional support

We are looking for photographs from women — mothers — who would be willing to share their family archives with me. The photographs can be anonymous; faces do not have to be visible. I will leave the selection up to you — what matters to me is that the photographs represent your motherhood. I would be very grateful if you could please send them to the following email address: eva.lammelova@gmail.com
If you have any questions, please feel free to write to me. I will be happy to answer anything.



Thank you.




ABOUT DIRECTOR

Eva Lammelová (born 1986)
Czech documentary filmmaker studied Sociology, Andragogy and Film/Theatre Studies at Palacký University Olomouc and Gender Studies at Utrecht University. She has directed and produced several TV documentaries and two independent ones, including AsexuaLOVE (One World 2017) and Anomaly (AFO, Ji.hlava 2019). She founded the DOKU Zahrady, documentary festival in her hometown Litomyšl.
