I am a documentary filmmaker working in both film and radio documentary production.
I hold a Master’s degree in Sociology / Andragogy from the Faculty of Arts at Palacký University in Olomouc, where I also completed a Bachelor’s degree in Film and Theatre Studies.
Since 2023, I have also been working as the Coordinator of Sign Language Courses at the non-profit organization Tichý svět.



PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE

  • 2002 – 2006 – Production work for Výtvarná Smetanova Litomyšl
  • 2010 – 2011 – Studies at Utrecht University, Netherlands – Sociology and Gender Studies
  • 2003 – 2013 – Work on exhibitions and events at Museum Kampa – Sovovy Mlýny, Prague
  • 2011 – Internship at Impakt, a non-profit organization focused on experimental audiovisual art, Utrecht (archival work and production of artistic events)
  • 2012 – 2013 – Production manager at the art studio Družina, Prague (community events, festival production, filming social campaigns and documentary films)
  • 2013 – 2014 – One-year internship at the Department of Social Documentary and Journalism (TPS) at Czech Television – assistant in dramaturgy and production of documentary films
  • 2013 – 2014 – Assistant director on the popular science children’s series about physics “I Will Be Einstein” (ČT:D)
  • 2014 – 2019 – Director of programmes Občanské noviny, Nedej se PLUS, and documentaries within the ecological series Nedej se (Czech Television, ČT2)
  • 2015 – 2016 – Collaboration with Inventura, a platform for artistic creation by people with intellectual disabilities (filming documentary portraits)
  • Since 2015, I have been directing television and independent documentary films (e.g. AsexuaLOVE – One World Festival, Czech Competition 2017; Anomálie – Academia Film Olomouc, Czech Competition 2019; screening at Ji.hlava International Documentary Film Festival 2019; Fascination: Exprmntl.cz).
  • Since 2016, I have been creating audio documentaries (Radio Wave, Czech Radio Dvojka, Vltava, Czech Radio Plus, Hatefree). My audio documentary Man, the Dog’s Best Friend won 2nd place at Prix Bohemia Radio 2019.
  • 2016 – 2019 – Documentary reports for the programme Průvan (ČT Art)
  • 2018 – 2021 – Founder and director of the authorial documentary film festival DOKU Zahrady in Litomyšl
  • 2018 – 2020 – Teaching at FAMO – course on film and audio documentary production for international students
  • 2021 – 2022 – Moderator of the documentary discussion webinar NEW ERA, Centre for Documentary Film, Ji.hlava International Documentary Film Festival
  • Since 2021 – Hosting film screenings and moderating debates with guests at Ji.hlava IDFF; also producing festival reports and studio interviews
  • 2021 – 2024 – Teaching practical audio documentary production at the Department of Theatre and Film Studies, Faculty of Arts, Palacký University Olomouc
  • 2022 – Field worker at the non-profit organization Sousedský klub Vršovice, Prague
  • 2023 – Film workshop for Prague Science Film Fest, Czech University of Life Sciences (production of a popular-science documentary with students)
  • 2023 – Summer reporting workshops for talented teenagers organized by JCMM, Brno
  • Since 2024 – Development of a documentary film about glass ceilings, supported by the Czech Audiovisual Fund
  • Since 2023, I have also been working as the Coordinator of Sign Language Courses at the non-profit organization Tichý svět.
